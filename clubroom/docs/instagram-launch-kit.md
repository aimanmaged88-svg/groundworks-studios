## Bio (150 characters max)

> Run your junior club off your phone. Regos, members, coaches, parents. Your logo, your colours. Bankstown-built. 30 days free 👇

(If you'd rather no emoji at all, end with "Link below.")

Link in bio: the preview builder, not the homepage. `https://<domain>/preview` — a club president who lands there types their club name and sees their own colours in under a minute. That's the hook.

## Highlights

2. **Real clubs** — South West Sonics first, with permission and no member data on screen.
3. **Behind it** — you, the story: three mates restarted a club, the group chat, the spreadsheet, the inbox.

## Captions, in your voice

Keep them short and specific. Name the thing (Friday, the group chat, U12s), skip slogans. One idea per post. Reply to every comment for the first month; that's the sales pipeline.

## What not to post

- Any real child's name, face or details. Demo club only, or a club that has signed off in writing.
- Screens with the dev badge or localhost URLs.
- Prices as promises before the pilot clubs have confirmed them.

## Assets

Screens are captured by the Playwright tests (`tests/e2e/screenshots/`) and the demo shots in `public/screens/`. Canva is connected in this workspace, so the carousel templates can be built there in the club-sheet look: Archivo Black headings, one colour band, hairline rules.
