Good Questions

Before you book

Are your pest treatments safe for kids and pets?

We use professional-grade products applied to label directions and we'll walk you through exactly what to do. For most treatments, that simply means keeping kids and pets off treated areas until they've dried.

Will pressure washing damage my roof, render or paint?

Not with us. Delicate surfaces like render, cladding, painted walls and roofs get a soft wash — a low-pressure clean that kills mould and lichen at the root without blasting the surface. High pressure is saved for concrete, pavers and machinery.

Do I need to be home?

For outdoor washing, usually not — we just need access to the area and an outside tap. For inside pest treatments, someone will need to let us in.

How do the "from" prices work?

Package prices are based on an average Sydney property. We confirm your final price before any work starts — never after. Larger homes, heavy build-up or extra areas can change the quote, and you'll always know first.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Sydney Metro — from the CBD to Penrith, and the Northern Beaches to Campbelltown. Use the suburb checker to confirm yours.

Do you work with businesses and strata?

Yes — homes, businesses and strata. We also wash trucks, fleets and heavy equipment on-site, and can set up regular service schedules. Get in touch for a custom quote.

How quickly can you come out?

Book online and we'll confirm the earliest available time. For urgent pest problems, call or WhatsApp us on 0416 224 292.
