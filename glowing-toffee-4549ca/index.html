How to make this with AI

The fast workflow — tap "Copy" on any block below.

AI video tools make short clips (5–10 sec), not whole episodes — and they can't really do mouths/lip-sync yet. So the play is:

  1. Make the character art first (next section) so everyone stays consistent.
  2. Generate shot by shot. Each scene has ready-to-paste shot prompts (visuals only — no talking).
  3. Add voices + sound after in CapCut. Use the Dialogue lines as voiceover or subtitles.

Tools that work well: Kling AI or Hailuo (great motion, generous free tier) or Runway / Pika for the video · ElevenLabs for AI voices · CapCut to stitch it together, add music + the title cards.

Too Loud

Exterior · Backyard

What happens

It's too much. The shouting, the bodies, the game gone sideways from how he pictured it — a shot rims out in the mess. The world warps: sounds smear and spike. Junaid clamps his hands over his ears and shuts his eyes, starting to shut down. The crew freezes, unsure. Then Asiya is the first to notice — she quietly comes and sits close beside him. Two autistic kids who just get each other, no words needed.

Dialogue

  • SOUND: (muffle + distort the audio here — Junaid's overwhelm POV)
  • JUNAID: (overwhelmed, quiet) "Too loud… too loud."
  • ASIYA: (soft reassuring sound, sits close beside him)
  • AMINA: (quietly) "…Did we do something?"

Reads the Game

Exterior · Backyard

Calm now, Junaid steps back in — on his terms. The yard settles into a fair game. His eyes scan the court and he sees the pattern nobody else can.
