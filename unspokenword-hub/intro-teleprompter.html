Spot 1 · The picture of health · the mask
Look: straight after the gym — pump on, clean and sharp, well-lit. Prim and proper on purpose: you look like you've got it all together. That polish IS the point — it's the mask the whole film takes off. Shoot at / just outside the gym, or a crisp, tidy space. ~50s (trim freely in the edit). No rush — film it whenever.

Take a good look at me. Gym done. Healthy. Put together — like I've got it all under control.

And I do take care of myself. Religiously. My body is the one thing I won't let slip.

But that's the outside. And the outside lies.

Inside, I'm struggling. I'm fighting something most people never see — every single day.

I don't numb it. No drink, no vape, no locking myself away. I heal the hard way, the natural way — because the second I stop, it wins.

I refuse to lose.

I'm not telling you this for sympathy. I'm telling you because I got here — and I don't want you to.

This is me reaching back — to show you the way out before you ever need it. The tips, the steps, the stuff that actually works.

I have a story to tell. And I just hope it's not too late for you — like it is for me.
