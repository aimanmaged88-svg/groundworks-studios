# Clutch HQ

Live at **https://clutch-hq.netlify.app**

Club operations for Clutch Basketball: every team, session, player, registration and fee in one place, for any number of teams.

The first visit asks how to start:

- **Start fresh**: an empty club with Camille Allam (Head coach, Finance, Admin) and a setup checklist: venues, coaches, teams, players, season dates.
- **Look around first**: a made-up demo club with 8 teams and 74 players. The mobile numbers come from the range ACMA reserves for fiction, and emails use example.com.

Switch between them any time in Settings. Everything saves in the browser on the device you're using, so each person has their own copy until the shared login version is built. Back up from Settings.

## What it does

- **This week**: every training and game for every team on one board, with what needs attention first: coach double-bookings, court clashes, games short on players, over-age players, Working With Children Checks, registrations waiting, roll calls not taken. Maghrib times show on each day (calculated from Sydney sunset, check the local masjid timetable).
- **Teams**: add a team in one form (age group, division, comp, coach, training slot, game night). It appears on the week board, in Money and in the roster lists straight away. The training squad holds players who train but aren't on a comp roster yet.
- **Players**: filter by team, fees owing, low attendance, over age, medical note or no photo consent. Each player has attendance, fees, parent contacts, notes and history. Export to Excel.
- **Registrations**: the website form's fields, as a pipeline: New, Contacted, Tryout booked, Offered a spot, Not now. Copy-ready WhatsApp messages for each step, and one click to put a player on a roster.
- **Money**: expected, collected and owing by team, with payment recording and reminder messages.
- **Coaches**: teams, weekly load, clashes and WWCC expiry.
- **Settings**: club, season dates, age-group year, roster minimums, venues, backup and restore.

## Age groups

Age on 31 December of the season's year: U12 is 11 or under, U14 is 12–13, U16 is 14–15, U18 is 16–17. The year is set in Settings.
