Who we are

A club built around the juniors.

South West Sonics started in 2024 and grew fast, because there wasn't enough of this out here. We're a junior club first — most of our kids come from right around Bankstown, and plenty walk in having never played a real game.

We keep it simple. Every coach is screened. Nobody gets cut for being new. And if the fee is ever the thing stopping a kid playing, you talk to us quietly and we sort it. Your kid, our club.
