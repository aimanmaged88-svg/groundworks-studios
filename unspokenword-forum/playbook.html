3 The ~10-min teach · talk-to-camera

Use your studio teleprompter. Topic: foods that regulate (swap if you like).

You know the 10am meltdown? For the longest time I treated it as behaviour. It wasn't — it was breakfast.

A fast, sugary start spikes blood sugar then drops it off a cliff, and the crash doesn't land as a tummy ache. It lands as dysregulation.

So we did one thing: protein first. Eggs, yoghurt, nut butter — before anything sweet. Steady fuel instead of a rollercoaster.

I'm not here to tell you what to feed your kid. Every body's different, and big changes are a chat for your GP or dietitian. I'm here to say: look at the fuel before you look at the child.

Pick ONE meal this week. Make it steadier. Watch what happens an hour later.

That's the whole teach. Now — I want your questions. The messy ones. There are none that are silly here.
