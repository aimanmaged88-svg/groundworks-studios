# Unspoken — Open Forum · Playbook

Everything to promote, run, and follow up the live forum. Copy-paste from here.
Format: **short teach (~10 min) → open Q&A**, hosted on **Zoom**.

Placeholders to swap: **[DAY DATE]**, **[TIME Sydney]** / **[TIME Lombok]**, **[REGISTER LINK]** (your Netlify register page), **[ZOOM LINK]** (set it in the Host console, not in posts).

---

## 1. Run-of-show (≈45–55 min)

| Time | You're doing | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| −10 min | Open the Zoom room early, cosy music, "we'll start at [TIME]" slide | Let people trickle in. Breathe. |
| 0:00 | **Welcome (2 min)** | "You're in the right place. No cameras required, no pressure to talk. Drop questions in the Live room link anytime." |
| 0:02 | **Ground rules (1 min)** | Kind space · lived experience not medical advice · what's shared here stays here. |
| 0:03 | **The teach (~10 min)** — *[TEACH TOPIC: Foods that regulate]* | One idea, usable this week. See script below. End with: "That's the spark — now it's yours." |
| 0:13 | **Open the floor (1 min)** | "Open the Live room tab, type a question or upvote one. I'll take the top-voted first." Pull up the Host console Q&A. |
| 0:14 | **Open Q&A (~30 min)** | Tap **Answering** on a question so it spotlights; **Answered** when done. Read quiet folks' typed questions aloud so they don't have to speak. |
| 0:44 | **Last calls (3 min)** | "Two more — anything we didn't get to, drop it and I'll cover it in a reel." |
| 0:47 | **Warm close (3 min)** | One takeaway each in the chat. Point them to the **reflection box** in the Live room. "DM me one word: what landed?" |
| 0:50 | **Stop recording, stay for stragglers** | Optional 5 min informal. |

**Moderator tips**
- Silence is fine. Count to five. Someone always fills it.
- If no questions at first, you seed one: "A common one I get is…" (pre-load 3 in the Live room before you start).
- Hard/triggering question → "Thank you for trusting us with that. Here's what helped us, and please loop in your GP."
- Keep a glass of water on. You set the regulated tone.

## 3. The ~10-minute teach — talk-to-camera script

Use your studio teleprompter. Topic: **foods that regulate** (swap if you like).

> You know the 10am meltdown? For the longest time I treated it as behaviour. It wasn't — it was breakfast.
>
> A fast, sugary start spikes blood sugar then drops it off a cliff, and the crash doesn't land as a tummy ache. It lands as dysregulation.
>
> So we did one thing: protein first. Eggs, yoghurt, nut butter — before anything sweet. Steady fuel instead of a rollercoaster.
>
> I'm not here to tell you what to feed your kid. Every body's different, and big changes are a chat for your GP or dietitian. I'm here to say: look at the fuel before you look at the child.
>
> Pick ONE meal this week. Make it steadier. Watch what happens an hour later. Keep a note on your phone.
>
> That's it. That's the whole teach. Now — I want your questions. The messy ones, the ones you're scared to ask. There are none that are silly here.

## 4. Promo pack

### 4a. Announcement reel (talk-to-camera, ~25s)
**Hook:** "I'm hosting something I've never done before — and I'm a little nervous."
**Body:** "An open forum. One hour, on Zoom, just us. I'll share a bit about foods that regulate, then it's yours — bring every question you've been too tired or too scared to ask. No cameras needed. No judgement. Just a room full of people who get it."
**CTA:** "Free. Link in bio to save your seat — [DAY DATE], [TIME Sydney]. Come be in the room. 💜"
**On-screen text beats:** "An open forum" / "1 hour · Zoom · free" / "bring your questions" / "save your seat 👆"

### 4b. Story slides (3)
1. *"I'm opening a room. 💜"* — plain text, your face or a calm photo.
2. *"[DAY], [TIME]. One hour on Zoom. A short teach on foods that regulate, then open Q&A — your questions, the real ones."*
3. *"Free. Tap to save your seat 👇"* + link sticker to **[REGISTER LINK]**.

### 4c. Countdown caption (post 2–3 days before)
> This [DAY] we're trying something new: an **open forum**. 💜
>
> One hour on Zoom. I'll open with ~10 min on foods that regulate — then it's yours. Bring the questions you've been carrying. Camera optional, judgement-free, actually warm.
>
> It's free. Save your seat (link in bio) and bring a friend who needs this room too.
>
> [DAY DATE] · [TIME Sydney] / [TIME Lombok]
>
> Not medical advice — just a community holding space for each other.

### 4d. Last-call caption (morning of)
> Tonight. 💜 The open forum is in a few hours — if you've been on the fence, this is your nudge. You don't have to speak, you don't need your camera. You just have to show up. Link in bio. See you in the room.

### 4e. After (recap, next day)
> Last night a room full of us sat together and asked the hard questions out loud. 💜 Thank you to everyone who came, typed, upvoted, and trusted us. Didn't make it? Tell me the question you'd have asked — I'll answer the top ones in reels this week. More of these coming.

4. Set status to **Live** when you start, **Ended** when you're done.
